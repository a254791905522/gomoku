Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 28, 2026

Overview

Starry Gomoku ("the App") is a single-player puzzle game developed for iOS. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

No Data Collection

The App does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App operates entirely offline and does not require an internet connection to function. We do not:

  • Collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personal identifiers
  • Track your location, device usage, or browsing activity
  • Share any information with third parties
  • Use any analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs

Local Data Storage

The App stores game progress locally on your device, including:

  • Completed levels and star ratings
  • Game settings and preferences

This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any external server. If you delete the App, all local game data will be permanently removed from your device.

No Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising networks, analytics platforms, social media SDKs, or other services that could collect user data.

Children's Privacy

The App is suitable for users of all ages. Since no personal data is collected, there are no privacy concerns specific to children. Parents can feel confident that their children's privacy is fully protected while using the App.

In-App Purchases & Payments

The App is completely free and does not offer any in-app purchases, subscriptions, or premium features. No payment information is ever requested or processed.

Changes to This Policy

If this Privacy Policy is updated, the changes will be posted on this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the App Store listing.

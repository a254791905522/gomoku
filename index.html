Starry Gomoku

Game Features

⭐

150 Puzzle Levels

Progressively challenging Gomoku puzzles across 150 unique levels. Master strategy and logic as you advance.

Puzzle-Based Gomoku

Not a traditional Gomoku — each level is a carefully designed puzzle. Place your pieces strategically to form five-in-a-row.

↩️

Undo & Hint System

Stuck on a level? Use Undo to reverse moves or Hint to get guidance. Practice makes perfect.

🌌

Space-Themed Design

Enjoy a beautiful cosmic aesthetic with stars, planets, and nebulae. Relaxing visuals for focused gameplay.

📱

Offline & Free

No ads, no in-app purchases, no login required. Play anytime, anywhere — completely free.

Star Rating System

Complete levels efficiently to earn up to 3 stars per level. Aim for a perfect score across all 150 levels.
